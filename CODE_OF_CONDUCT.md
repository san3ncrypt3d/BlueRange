# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint. Be respectful, accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the community, and avoid harassment, threats, sexualised attention, or publishing private information.

Project maintainers may remove or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently ban participants for conduct they deem inappropriate. Report conduct concerns through repository maintainer contact mechanisms. Reports will be handled as confidentially and fairly as practical. This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
