## TITLE
BlueRange: Reproducibility Data and Artifact for Authority-Aware Evaluation of Autonomous LLM Cyber Defenders

## CREATOR
Sanjay Babu

## DESCRIPTION
BlueRange is an open-source benchmark for evaluating the defensive effectiveness and operational safety of autonomous LLM-based cyber defenders under explicit authority constraints. This artifact contains curated experimental data, provenance metadata, and reproducibility material associated with the study "BlueRange: Evaluating the Safety–Effectiveness Trade-off of Autonomous LLM Cyber Defenders."

The release contains canonical data supporting the reported Scenario 1 and Scenario 2 results, including attack detection, correct containment, benign specificity, false belief, false containment, authority-level outcomes, evidence-profile observations, and protocol-reliability measurements.

The release is intended to support inspection and reproduction of the reported aggregate results. It does not contain the manuscript itself, private execution infrastructure, credentials, or the complete private raw research archive. Provider-mediated LLM execution may not be byte-for-byte reproducible because of model/runtime availability and provider nondeterminism.
